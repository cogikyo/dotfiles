# Design

Work within the product's intent and existing visual language.
Read the project's design guide first: **`DESIGN.md` overrides this family's defaults**.
Apply a project copy guide to words as well.

> [!INFO] Attention
>
> **Attention is a fixed budget.**
>
> - Every element and every unit of emphasis spends it.
> - Spend it on what the user needs to understand or do.

*More things to read or notice make the important things harder to find.*
Presence itself signals importance; adding emphasis increases that claim.

## The test

- Treat each element as **unnecessary until proven essential**.
- Ask: **would the reader miss it if it were gone?**
  - If not, delete it.
  - If so, keep the meaning and remove treatment that does no useful work.
- Judge the **move**, not a catalog of forbidden shapes.
  - The subskills' clutter tells are prompts to inspect purpose, not automatic defects.
  - *Bans only relocate clutter when the same habit survives in another form.*

## Floor

- Keep information that prevents a **real mistake** or explains a non-obvious consequence.
- Keep **accessible names**, needed affordances, and meaning visible at the point of use.
  - Do not hide task-essential meaning in tooltips.
- Keep **hierarchy** and deliberate character.
  - Giving everything the same size and weight makes importance harder to see.
  - *Restraint must leave the product understandable and usable.*

## Load the relevant subskills

Load this parent before any subskill; combine only those the change touches.

- **`copy`** (`copy/SKILL.md`): words, labels, claims, messages, and repeated text on any product surface.
- **`hierarchy`** (`hierarchy/SKILL.md`): size, weight, relative contrast, position, spacing, grouping, and structural chrome.
- **`color`** (`color/SKILL.md`): palette, accents, saturation, color meaning, and accessible color use.
- **`motion`** (`motion/SKILL.md`): animation, transitions, activity indicators, and repeated movement.

Stay within the approved scope; a copy pass does not authorize a layout or behavior change.
When evidence or scope is missing, return the question instead of inventing intent.
